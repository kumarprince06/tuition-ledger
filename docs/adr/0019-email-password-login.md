# ADR-0019 Email + password as a second sign-in method alongside WhatsApp codes
**Status.** Accepted. Partly supersedes ADR-0009 (which chose WhatsApp codes only).
**Context.** Owners register their centre from a laptop and expect email and password, as in every other business tool; some owners and accountants work from a desktop where the WhatsApp phone isn't at hand. ADR-0009 rejected passwords because staff share them and email is rare among teachers. Both concerns are still real, so passwords are added, not substituted.
**Decision.** Registration takes coaching centre name, owner name, email, WhatsApp number and password (CEN-6). Every user can sign in either with a WhatsApp code (unchanged from ADR-0009) or with email + password (CEN-8). Passwords: at least 8 characters, checked against a common/breached list, stored as Argon2id. Five failures in 15 minutes lock password sign-in for 15 minutes and notify the user on WhatsApp; the WhatsApp code still works. Email sign-in needs a verified email. Reset by a single-use email link (30 minutes) or a WhatsApp code; reset and password change revoke all sessions. Staff invited by phone may add an email and password later from My account. Tokens are unchanged: 15-minute access JWT, rotated 30-day refresh cookie.
**Consequences.** New endpoints `POST /auth/login`, `POST /auth/email/verify`, `POST /auth/password/forgot`, `POST /auth/password/reset`, `PATCH /me/credentials`; `users.email` (unique, case-insensitive), `email_verified_at`, `password_hash`, `failed_logins`, `locked_until`; `auth_tokens` table. A transactional email provider becomes a third external service (risk R16). More attack surface (credential stuffing), handled by the controls in 11-security-privacy.md. Lost-phone recovery no longer depends only on the owner: a user with a verified email can still sign in.
**Alternatives.** Passwords only: loses the zero-friction phone flow for teachers. WhatsApp codes only (ADR-0009): blocks laptop-first owners and desktop accountants. Magic links by email: depends on email deliverability for every sign-in.
