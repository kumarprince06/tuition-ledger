# ADR-0018 Self-serve centre registration with OTP-verified owner; WhatsApp sender connected during onboarding
**Context.** 08-saas-plans.md promises a 30-day Starter trial for new centres, but centres could only be created from the platform console (PLT-1), so growth depended on Tuition Ledger staff. Parents must see the centre's own name on WhatsApp, never "Tuition Ledger" (01-prd.md §5), and a WhatsApp Business number needs Meta approval that takes days.
**Decision.** A centre registers itself (CEN-6): the owner gives coaching centre name, owner name, email, WhatsApp number and password (ADR-0019); the receipt prefix is generated from the name and can be changed in Settings until the first receipt, so registration asks one question less; the WhatsApp number is verified by OTP before any row is created, and the email is verified by a link. The same provisioning service backs the platform console. One phone number and one email are one account (both unique). The centre's WhatsApp sender is connected as onboarding step 2; until it is approved the whole app works and parent messages are stored in the outbox as `held`, then released (CEN-7). Email is also used for invoices and platform notices.
**Consequences.** New endpoints `POST /signup/otp`, `POST /signup`, `GET /centre/whatsapp`; `centres.whatsapp_status`, `trial_ends_on`, `signup_source`; `users.email` and the credential columns from ADR-0019. A fresh centre can enter data on day one but cannot message parents until approval (R14). Abuse is bounded by OTP-first, 3 registrations per phone and per IP per day, and console flags (R15). A teacher who works at two centres needs two numbers until users and memberships are split (revisit with CEN-5).
**Alternatives.** Console-only creation: no self-serve trials. A shared Tuition Ledger sender during the trial: breaks the "centre's own name" rule and confuses parents when the number changes. Phone-only registration (the first draft of this ADR): replaced by ADR-0019 so laptop-first owners can use email and password.
