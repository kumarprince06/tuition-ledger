# ADR-0021 Parent fees settle only to the centre's own accounts
**Status.** Accepted.
**Context.** CEN-1 gives each centre a "payment account" but never said how it is connected, and 03 §5 verified Razorpay webhooks against one secret per environment, which implies a single Razorpay account. Collecting parents' fees in Tuition Ledger's account and paying centres later would make Tuition Ledger a payment aggregator (RBI authorisation) and liable for refunds, chargebacks and tax on every centre's money. The PRD already excludes storing card or bank credentials.
**Decision.** Parent fees go only to the centre: cash, a UPI payment to the centre's own UPI ID, or a payment link on the centre's own Razorpay account. There is never a fallback to Tuition Ledger's account; a centre without Razorpay has no payment-link button. Tuition Ledger's own Razorpay account is used only to bill centres for their plan (08 §3).

- **UPI (default, free).** The owner enters `centres.upi_id` and `upi_payee_name` in Settings. Reminders and the desk QR use a standard link `upi://pay?pa=<upi_id>&pn=<payee>&am=<amount>&tn=<student>-<period>`. There is no provider callback; the payment is recorded by UTR entry (PAY-3).
- **Razorpay (optional, automatic confirmation).** Phase 1: the owner pastes the key id and key secret and a webhook secret into Settings. Phase 2: "Connect Razorpay" through Razorpay Partner OAuth once Tuition Ledger is approved; the stored credential becomes an access and refresh token. Credentials live in `centre_payment_accounts` (tenant table, row-level security), encrypted with AES-GCM under a key held outside the database (env), never returned by the API (only `rzp_live_••••AB12`), writable by OWNER only and audited.
- **Webhooks.** Each centre has its own endpoint `POST /webhooks/razorpay/{centreId}`, verified with that centre's webhook secret; `notes.centre_id` on the link must match the path. Payment links carry `reference_id` (our link id) and `notes` (centre, student, charges) so a payment maps to its charges without guessing. A job every 15 minutes polls open links older than 10 minutes in case a webhook was lost; both paths call `PaymentService.record` with the Razorpay payment id as the idempotency key (ADR-0006).

**Consequences.** Tuition Ledger never holds parent money. Every centre can collect by UPI from day one at no fee; automatic confirmation needs the centre's own Razorpay KYC (about a week). We hold sensitive per-centre secrets until OAuth replaces them, so key rotation, masking and audit are required. One webhook secret per centre instead of per environment.
**Alternatives.** Fallback to Tuition Ledger's Razorpay account: aggregator licensing and liability. Razorpay Route (linked accounts under our account): money still passes through our account first. OAuth only from day one: blocks Razorpay for every centre until the partner application is approved.
