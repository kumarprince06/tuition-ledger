# ADR-0016 Test results and assessments as structured templates, one child per message, no ranks
**Context.** Centres want to send marks and monthly progress to parents. Marks are personal data about a minor; WhatsApp requires approved templates outside the 24-hour window; posting marks in a group is the behaviour we replace.
**Decision.** Tests and marks are first-class data, not free text. Results go out as structured template messages, one message per child to the consenting parent, with the batch average optional and a rank never included. Sending is an explicit, logged action, once per test; corrections notify only the affected parent. The monthly assessment is generated from stored data (attendance, tests, a bounded teacher comment) and reviewed by the owner before sending when the centre requires it. Marks history is append-only like attendance.
**Consequences.** Parents get accurate, private progress updates in the channel they already use; teachers stop typing marks into groups; the owner can prove what was sent and when. Cost: two more templates to approve and a marks screen that must be fast on a phone.
**Alternatives.** Free-text messages: blocked by WhatsApp policy and unbounded. Ranks in messages: harmful to children and a complaint magnet. PDF report cards: out of MVP; the message is the report card.
