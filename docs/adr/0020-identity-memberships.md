# ADR-0020 People are global users; their role at a centre is a membership
**Status.** Accepted. Replaces `users.centre_id` and `users.role` from 05-data-model.md; resolves the "two numbers" limitation in ADR-0018; implements CEN-5 for every staff role.
**Context.** Teachers and accountants often work at two or three coaching centres, and an owner may open a second centre. With `users.centre_id` and unique phone and email, such a person needs a second phone number and a second password. Platform admins (Tuition Ledger staff) belong to no centre, so they either break `users.centre_id NOT NULL` or need a second user system. Login must find a person before any centre is known, which row-level security on a tenant-scoped `users` table would block.
**Decision.** `users` holds only the person: name, phone (unique), email (unique), credentials and `token_version`. It has no `centre_id` and no row-level security, and only the tenancy module may read it. `memberships(user_id, centre_id, role, status)` holds where the person works and as what; it is a tenant table with row-level security, one row per user per centre. Platform admins are ordinary users with a row in `platform_admins(user_id)`; they reach only `/platform/*` and hold no membership by default. Sign-in (WhatsApp code or email + password) identifies the user; if they have one active membership the token is issued for that centre, otherwise the response lists the centres and the SPA calls `POST /auth/centre` with the chosen one. The access token carries `{sub, centreId, role, ver}` for one centre; switching centre issues a new token. Inviting a phone or email that already belongs to a user adds a membership instead of a new user. Deactivation and role change act on the membership and bump `users.token_version`.
**Consequences.** One login per person across every centre. Each centre's staff list, staff limit (08 §1) and permissions come from its own memberships, so centre A never sees that a teacher also works at centre B. The tenant context is always a single centre, so isolation (ADR-0002) is unchanged. Risk: a careless query on `users` could list staff from every centre; mitigated by the module boundary (a Modulith test forbids access to `users` outside tenancy) and by `users` holding no student or money data. A `token_version` bump signs the person out of all centres, not only the one that changed; acceptable for a 15-minute access token.
**Alternatives.** `users.centre_id` plus a separate `platform_users` table: solves platform admins only; one person still cannot work at two centres. `users.centre_id` nullable for admins: needs an `IS NULL` exception in the RLS policy that leaks admin rows into every centre's staff list and staff count. Platform admins as members of a fake "platform centre": every centre listing and job must skip it, and the admin's job is cross-centre by nature. Company SSO for the console: a good later change to how admins sign in; it does not change these tables.
